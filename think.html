Think

Ideas, frameworks & perspectives.

The research territory: how context shapes human capacity, and how AI-mediated environments can be designed to preserve — rather than erode — human agency.

Ideas

Contextual well-being Human capacity AI & cognitive environments Agency-preserving AI AI governance as well-being efficacy Adaptive feedback systems

These are the concepts that recur across the research below — read individually or as a connected territory.

Frameworks

"My work focuses on addressing the gap between human decision-making capacity and AI-mediated environments by designing systems that preserve agency, clarity, and effective action."

System Overview

Input

Behavioural, contextual, and longitudinal well-being data from social microcosms.

Process

Modelling well-being as a functional capacity via the Spectrum of Contextual Well-being (SCW).

Output

Adaptive feedback mechanisms and agency-preserving intervention strategies.

Spectrum of Contextual Well-being

A functional framework mapping perception and judgment across complex environments.

AI Governance & Agency

Reframing human oversight as a dimension of well-being efficacy rather than just compliance.

AI Contextual Framework
