About

Researcher, founder,
and writer.

Yao is a PhD scholar at University College Dublin working at the intersection of Human-Computer Interaction (HCI), AI, and healthcare. She designs AI systems that preserve human agency and support decision-making in high-stakes environments.

She develops original conceptual frameworks and translates them into functional, AI-supported systems, bridging theory and implementation. As founder of ContextWell Lab, she leads work connecting research and applied contexts, with experience spanning language education and digital health projects.

Approach

What I study: how context shapes human capacity.
Why: because the environments people live, learn and work in shape what they're able to do — and can be redesigned.
How I think: interdisciplinary, systems-oriented, human-centred.

What I'm building: research initiatives, systems and platforms — starting with ContextWell.
Where I work: at the intersection of health, technology, human behaviour, education, public health and systems.

Current work

PhD research at University College Dublin, and founder & director of ContextWell Lab — an independent research initiative applying the Spectrum of Contextual Well-being to real systems.

Selected experience
